The Aurogel history

We began in 2019 with a single idea: fragrance should feel like skincare. Our chemists replaced alcohol carriers with a water-rich hydrogel base that hydrates while it diffuses scent.

Today, we blend minimalist design, soft tones, and clean formulations inspired by Rhode's refined aesthetic.
